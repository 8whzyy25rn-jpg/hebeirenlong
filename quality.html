Quality control

How each batch is checked

Goods are made to the standard they were ordered to, and the test results travel with the shipment. If your project needs a specific test or a third-party witness, say so at enquiry stage and it is written into the order.

In-house inspection and testing

Equipment available in our own laboratory and inspection room.

CheckWhat it covers
Tensile testTensile strength, yield strength and elongation of the batch material.
Impact testCharpy impact energy, where the material grade or the client calls for it.
Bend testDuctility of welds and of formed fittings.
Hydrostatic testPressure tightness of finished fittings and pipe on request.
Ultrasonic thicknessWall thickness at the ordered points, logged against the drawing.
SpectrometerChemical composition, to confirm the heat is the grade ordered.
Three-element analyserCarbon, silicon and manganese control in the melt.
Metallographic microscopeMicrostructure where the specification requires it.
X-ray inspectionWeld soundness for welded fittings and pipe, where ordered.
Certificates

Paperwork that travels with the goods

Material test certificate

EN 10204 3.1 per batch, with chemistry and mechanical results and a heat number that matches the marking on the goods.

Inspection reports

Dimensional records, thickness readings, and test reports for any test written into the order.

Shipping documents

Packing list, commercial invoice and, where required, certificate of origin and marking as agreed.

Quality documents on request

Each batch is supplied with an EN 10204 3.1 material test certificate and the inspection records for the tests written into the order. Further quality documents are available on request with a quotation.

Marking and packing

How the goods arrive

Marking

Size, wall or class, material grade and heat number are marked on the goods. Client marks and shipping marks are applied where the order specifies them.

Packing

Bundled, crated or palletised for sea freight, with timber treated to the destination country's requirements. Export seaworthy packing is standard.

Loading

We load at Huanghua Port and other North China ports, and can consolidate fittings, flanges, caps and pipe into one shipment.
