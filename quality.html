Quality control

How each batch is checked

Each batch is made to the ordered standard and tested in our own lab. Results travel with the shipment.

In-house inspection and testing

CheckWhat it covers
Tensile testTensile strength, yield strength and elongation of the batch material.
Impact testCharpy impact energy, where the material grade or the client calls for it.
Bend testDuctility of welds and of formed fittings.
Hydrostatic testPressure tightness of finished fittings and pipe on request.
Ultrasonic thicknessWall thickness at the ordered points, logged against the drawing.
SpectrometerChemical composition, to confirm the heat is the grade ordered.
