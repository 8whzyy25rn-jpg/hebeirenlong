What to send

The more complete the enquiry, the firmer the first price.

  1. Item and quantity — for example 200 pcs of 90° elbow.
  2. Size, wall thickness or pressure class.
  3. Standard, if it is fixed: ASME B16.9, EN 10253, ASME B16.5 and so on.
  4. Material grade: A234 WPB, A105, 304, 316, and so on.
  5. Coating, marking and packing requirements.
  6. Destination port or address, and the delivery date you are working to.
How the order runs

From enquiry to loading

  1. You send sizes, standards and quantities. Drawings are welcome for non-standard items.
  2. We quote item by item, with the standard, material, packing and certificate listed per line.
  3. Specification and, where relevant, the drawing are confirmed before production starts.
  4. Production, then in-house inspection with the test records issued against the batch.
  5. Marking, packing and loading at Huanghua Port or another North China port.
  6. Documents sent ahead of arrival: material certificates, packing list, invoice and origin papers.
