Butt-welding fittings

Long radius and short radius elbows, bends, equal and reducing tees, laterals, crosses, concentric and eccentric reducers, caps and dished heads, in seamless and welded construction. Ends are bevelled for welding and every piece is marked so it can be traced back to the heat.

  • Sizes1/2" – 48" (DN15 – DN1200)
  • Angles45°, 90°, 180°; 1.5D and 1D
  • StandardsASME B16.9, MSS SP-75, EN 10253, GB/T 12459
  • MaterialsA234 WPB, WPHY 52 / 70, A403 WP304 / 316 / 321
  • WallSCH10 – SCH160, XXS
  • TestsTensile, impact, bend, hydrostatic; EN 10204 3.1

Flanges

Weld neck, slip-on, blind, lapped and plate flanges, plus threaded types, in forged and rolled construction. Machined to the ordered standard, supplied with rust-preventive oil, primer or hot-dip galvanizing as required.

  • TypesWN, SO, BL, LJ, plate, threaded
  • PressureClass 150 – 2500; PN6 – PN40
  • StandardsASME B16.5 / B16.47, EN 1092-1, DIN, JIS, GB/T 9119
  • MaterialsQ235, 20#, 16Mn, A105, F304 / F316
  • FacingRF, FF, RTJ
  • FinishAnti-rust oil, primer, hot-dip galvanized

Forged socket-weld and threaded fittings

Small-bore fittings for instrument and utility lines: elbows, tees, crosses, couplings, caps, unions and plugs. Threads are cut to the ordered standard and gauged; socket bores are checked for depth and concentricity.

  • Sizes1/8" – 4"
  • Classes3000 LB, 6000 LB, SCH80
  • StandardsASME B16.11
  • MaterialsA105, F304, F316
  • EndsSocket-weld, NPT / BSP threaded
  • MarkingSize, class, material, heat number

Steel pipes

Seamless and welded pipe in carbon and stainless steel, supplied bare or hot-dip galvanized. Pipe is cut and bevelled to length where the order calls for it, and can be coated for buried or corrosive service.

  • SizesDN15 – DN2000
  • TypeSeamless, welded, galvanized
  • StandardsAPI 5L, ASTM A106 / A53 / A312, GB/T 9711
  • MaterialsCarbon steel, 304 / 316 stainless
  • Coating3PE, epoxy, hot-dip galvanized
  • EndsPlain, bevelled, threaded and coupled

Dished heads and caps

Elliptical, torispherical and hemispherical heads, plus pipe caps, formed to the ordered dimensions and supplied with the matching edge preparation.

  • FormsElliptical, torispherical, hemispherical
  • SizesTo drawing or to ASME / GB tables
  • MaterialsCarbon steel, stainless, alloy
  • EdgeAs ordered
